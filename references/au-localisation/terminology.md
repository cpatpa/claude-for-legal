# US to AU Legal Terminology

> [!CAUTION]
> **AI-generated mapping, not verified.** Many terms have nuanced differences that this table does not capture. Use as a translation starting point and check the AU concept against authoritative sources for substantive use.

| US | AU | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Attorney | Solicitor, barrister, legal practitioner, lawyer | Australia distinguishes solicitors (advise, transact, instruct) and barristers (advocacy, opinions). | Esquire / Esq. | (not used) | No Australian post-nominal equivalent. |
| Counsel | Counsel | Used; usually means barrister. "Senior Counsel" (SC) or "King's Counsel" (KC) for senior barristers. |

## Privilege and evidence

| US | AU | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Attorney-client privilege | Legal advice privilege (a limb of legal professional privilege) | See `privilege.md`. |
| Work product privilege / doctrine | (No direct equivalent) | Litigation privilege (other limb of LPP) is narrower than US work product. |
| Common interest privilege | Common interest privilege | Recognised but narrower. |
| FRE 408 (settlement) | Without prejudice privilege, Calderbank offers | Calderbank offers carry costs consequences distinct from US Rule 68 offers of judgment. |
| Privileged & Confidential | Privileged & Confidential | Marking is conventional; privilege depends on facts. |
| Attorney work product header | "Confidential and subject to legal professional privilege" or "Confidential" | Avoid asserting "work product" in AU contexts. |

## Substantive law concepts

| US | AU | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| At-will employment | (Not recognised) | Australia has minimum notice periods under NES (1-5 weeks) and unfair dismissal protections for employees with required minimum employment period. |
| Tortious interference | Inducing breach of contract; conspiracy by unlawful means | |
| Negligent infliction of emotional distress | Negligence causing psychiatric injury (recognised psychiatric illness required) | |
| Punitive damages | Exemplary damages, aggravated damages | More restricted; not available in defamation in some states; uncertain availability in contract. |
| Wrongful termination | Unfair dismissal (FWC), unlawful dismissal / adverse action (general protections, Fair Work Act Part 3-1) | Statutory schemes, not common law unjust dismissal. |
| Promissory estoppel | Promissory estoppel, equitable estoppel (Walton's Stores) | Doctrinally similar; one unified estoppel in some treatments. |
| Specific performance | Specific performance | |
| Lien | Lien | |
| Stop notice (construction) | Stop work order, security of payment claim (state SOP Acts) | |
| Mechanics lien | Sub-contractor charge, builder's lien | State-by-state security of payment regimes (e.g. *Building and Construction Industry Security of Payment Act 1999* (NSW)). |
| Diversity jurisdiction | (Not applicable) | Federation works differently. Federal Court jurisdiction is by subject matter, not diversity. |
| Forum non conveniens | Stay on grounds of forum non conveniens (clearly inappropriate forum test) | *Voth v Manildra Flour Mills* test, narrower than US *Gulf Oil* test. |
