# Dates, Currency, Spelling, Units

> [!CAUTION]
> **AI-generated conventions guide.** Verify any specific convention against current style guides if it matters for filed work.

Cross-cutting conventions that every Australian-localised skill should apply.

## Spelling: en-AU

Use Australian English consistently. Key conventions:

- `-ise` not `-ize`: organise, recognise, customise, prioritise, optimise. Note: words derived from Greek `-izein` use `-ise` in AU (organise) while a few words retain `-ize` regardless (capsize). When in doubt, prefer `-ise`.
- `-our` not `-or`: colour, behaviour, labour (except "Australian Labor Party", which uses the en-US spelling by historical convention).
- `-ce` for nouns, `-se` for verbs of the same root: licence (n), license (v); practice (n), practise (v); defence (n), defend (v).
- `-re` not `-er`: centre, theatre, metre (the unit; "meter" remains for measuring devices).
- `-ll-` in doubled-consonant inflections: travelling, modelling, labelled, counselled.
- `-ogue` not `-og`: catalogue, dialogue.
- "judgment" (no "e" after "g") is the conventional AU legal spelling in citations and titles of judicial decisions, but "judgement" (with "e") is also used in non-legal AU English.
- "memorandum" (singular), "memoranda" (plural) in legal writing; "memos" colloquially.

Some terms that follow specifically Australian usage:
- "rostered" (not "scheduled" in employment context).
- "annual leave" (not "vacation" or "PTO").
- "long service leave" (a uniquely Australian / NZ concept).
- "redundancy" (not "layoff" or "RIF").
- "termination" / "dismissal" (rather than "firing").
- "industrial action" (not "strike action" as the umbrella).
- "casual employee", "permanent employee" (specific AU categories).
- "ABN", "ACN", "TFN" (Australian Business Number, Australian Company Number, Tax File Number).
- "GST" (not "VAT" or "sales tax"): 10% on most goods and services.
- "Medicare" (the AU universal healthcare scheme, not the US senior healthcare scheme).
- "the Crown" (not "the government" where the constitutional formality matters).

## Dates

Format: **DD/MM/YYYY** or **DD Month YYYY**.

- Short: 15/05/2026
- Long: 15 May 2026
- Avoid ambiguous formats like 05/15/2026 (US) or 2026-05-15 (ISO is fine in technical contexts but not for legal correspondence).
- Day of week + date: "Monday, 15 May 2026" (comma after the day of week).
- Date ranges: "15 to 20 May 2026" or "15-20 May 2026".

Financial year: Australian financial year runs **1 July to 30 June**. FY26 means the year ending 30 June 2026.

Quarters: Q1 = July-September, Q2 = October-December, Q3 = January-March, Q4 = April-June (for FY-aligned reporting). Some entities follow calendar quarters; check the entity.

## Currency

Default to **AUD**.

- Symbol: `$` in AU-only contexts. Use `A$` or `AUD` where ambiguity with USD is possible (e.g. cross-border contracts, financial reports).
- Thousands separator: comma (`A$1,234,567.89`).
- Decimal: period (`.50`).
- GST: typically quoted as "ex GST" or "incl GST". The default for B2B is often ex GST; consumer pricing must be incl GST under the Australian Consumer Law (with limited exceptions).
- When converting from USD references in original US-default content, do not auto-convert. Note in localised content that "the AU equivalent depends on FX at the relevant date" and use round AUD figures where setting thresholds.

Common Australian regulatory thresholds (verify against current source):
- ASIC small proprietary company test (must satisfy 2 of 3): consolidated revenue < A$50M, consolidated gross assets < A$25M, fewer than 100 employees at year end. (Updated 2019 from earlier thresholds.)
- ACCC unfair contract terms small business threshold: fewer than 100 employees OR annual turnover < A$10M (as updated by the 2023 reforms).
- ACL "small amount claim" in some state tribunals: varies, commonly up to A$25,000.
- High Court of Australia special leave threshold for civil: discretionary; quantum is not a formal threshold.

## Units

Australia uses the metric system across all formal contexts.

- Length: mm, cm, m, km. Not inches, feet, miles.
- Area: m², ha, km². Not acres.
- Volume: mL, L, m³. Not gallons.
- Mass: g, kg, t. Not pounds, ounces.
- Temperature: °C. Not °F.
- Speed: km/h. Not mph.
- Fuel economy: L/100km (not mpg).

Where US-default content references US units, convert to metric when localising. Do not retain the US unit in parentheses unless precision matters and the context demands it.

## Phone numbers

Format: `+61 2 1234 5678` (international) or `(02) 1234 5678` (domestic).
Mobile: `+61 4XX XXX XXX` or `04XX XXX XXX`.

## Addresses

Format:
```
Recipient Name
Unit/Level + Street number Street name
Suburb STATE POSTCODE
AUSTRALIA
```

Example:
```
Jane Smith
Level 35, 1 Macquarie Street
SYDNEY NSW 2000
AUSTRALIA
```

State abbreviations in addresses (upper case): NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT, NT.
Note: in AGLC citations, the convention is mixed case (NSW, Vic, Qld, WA, SA, Tas, ACT, NT). See `citation-aglc.md`.
