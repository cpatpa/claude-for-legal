## Commonwealth (federal) regulators

### ASIC: Australian Securities and Investments Commission
- **Scope**: Corporations Act 2001 (Cth), ASIC Act 2001 (Cth), National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth), financial services licensing (AFSL), credit licensing (ACL), markets supervision, registered company auditors and liquidators, financial product disclosure.
- **Key obligations**: Directors' duties (Corporations Act ss 180-184), continuous disclosure (s 674), AFSL conditions, breach reporting (s 912DAA reportable situations regime), DDO (design and distribution obligations).
- **Enforcement**: Civil penalty proceedings (Federal Court), criminal referral to CDPP, banning orders, infringement notices, public examinations under s 19 ASIC Act.
- **Website**: asic.gov.au

### ACCC: Australian Competition and Consumer Commission
- **Scope**: Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) including the Australian Consumer Law (Schedule 2). Cartels, misuse of market power, misleading and deceptive conduct, unconscionable conduct, unfair contract terms, product safety, mergers (informal and formal authorisation regimes), industry codes.
- **Key obligations**: ACL s 18 (misleading or deceptive conduct), ACL ss 20-22 (unconscionable conduct), unfair contract terms regime (substantially expanded November 2023, applies to standard form contracts), consumer guarantees (ACL ss 51-59), mandatory product safety standards.
- **Enforcement**: Civil penalty proceedings (Federal Court), infringement notices, court-enforceable undertakings, public warning notices.
- **Note on unfair contract terms**: Since 9 November 2023, unfair terms in standard form consumer and small business contracts attract civil penalties (previously the term was just void). Max penalty per breach for companies is the greater of $50M, 3x benefit, or 30% of adjusted turnover.
- **Website**: accc.gov.au

### OAIC: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner
- **Scope**: Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Freedom of Information Act 1982 (Cth), Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, Consumer Data Right (with ACCC).
- **Key obligations**: 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), Notifiable Data Breaches scheme (Privacy Act Part IIIC), Privacy Impact Assessments for Commonwealth agencies (mandatory for high privacy risk projects).
- **Enforcement**: Civil penalty applications (Federal Court), enforceable undertakings, determinations, conciliation. Max penalty for serious or repeated interference with privacy: greater of $50M, 3x benefit, or 30% of adjusted turnover (Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024).
- **Website**: oaic.gov.au

### Fair Work Commission and Fair Work Ombudsman
- **Fair Work Commission (FWC)**: Independent tribunal. Unfair dismissal, general protections (adverse action), industrial action, modern awards review, enterprise agreement approval, anti-bullying and sexual harassment jurisdictions.
- **Fair Work Ombudsman (FWO)**: Enforcement of Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth), modern awards, NES. Underpayment investigations, compliance notices, Federal Court proceedings.
- **Website**: fwc.gov.au, fairwork.gov.au

### AHRC: Australian Human Rights Commission
- **Scope**: Sex Discrimination Act 1984 (Cth), Racial Discrimination Act 1975 (Cth), Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth), Age Discrimination Act 2004 (Cth), Australian Human Rights Commission Act 1986 (Cth).
- **Process**: Complaints lodged with AHRC, conciliation, then if unresolved, complainant can apply to Federal Court or Federal Circuit and Family Court. Note: positive duty to eliminate sexual harassment, sex discrimination, and victimisation introduced by Respect at Work amendments (in force 12 December 2023). AHRC has compliance and investigation powers from 12 December 2023.
- **Website**: humanrights.gov.au

### IP Australia
- **Scope**: Patents Act 1990 (Cth), Trade Marks Act 1995 (Cth), Designs Act 2003 (Cth), Plant Breeder's Rights Act 1994 (Cth).
- **Functions**: Registration, examination, opposition, hearings. Not an enforcement body, infringement proceedings run in the Federal Court.
- **Website**: ipaustralia.gov.au

### APRA: Australian Prudential Regulation Authority
- **Scope**: Authorised deposit-taking institutions (ADIs / banks), general and life insurers, private health insurers, superannuation funds (most). Prudential standards (CPS, APS, LPS, HPS, SPS series).
- **Notable**: CPS 234 (information security), CPS 230 (operational risk, commenced 1 July 2025), prudential approach to climate-related financial risk.
- **Website**: apra.gov.au

### AUSTRAC: Australian Transaction Reports and Analysis Centre
- **Scope**: Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (Cth). Reporting entities must register, conduct customer due diligence, submit suspicious matter reports, threshold transaction reports, international funds transfer instructions.
- **2024-2025 expansion**: "Tranche 2" amendments extend AML/CTF obligations to lawyers, accountants, trust and company service providers, real estate professionals, and dealers in precious stones and metals from 1 July 2026.
- **Website**: austrac.gov.au

### ATO: Australian Taxation Office
- **Scope**: Income tax, GST, FBT, PAYG, superannuation guarantee, transfer pricing, tax administration. Tax Practitioners Board (separate body) regulates tax agents.
- **Website**: ato.gov.au

### AFCA: Australian Financial Complaints Authority
- **Scope**: External dispute resolution scheme for financial services complaints (banking, insurance, superannuation, investments, credit). Members include all AFSL holders and credit licensees.
- **Binding nature**: AFCA determinations binding on the financial firm if the complainant accepts. Complainant retains right to court.
- **Website**: afca.org.au

### Other Commonwealth bodies of note
- **eSafety Commissioner**: Online Safety Act 2021 (Cth). Cyberbullying, image-based abuse, abhorrent violent material, basic online safety expectations.
- **ACMA**: Australian Communications and Media Authority. Broadcasting, telecommunications, online content (with eSafety), spam (Spam Act 2003), do not call register.
- **TGA**: Therapeutic Goods Administration. Medicines, medical devices, advertising of therapeutic goods.
- **CASA**: Civil Aviation Safety Authority.
- **NAATI**: National Accreditation Authority for Translators and Interpreters.
- **ART**: Administrative Review Tribunal (replaced AAT on 14 October 2024). Merits review of Commonwealth administrative decisions.

## State and territory regulators (high level)

- **Fair Trading / Consumer Affairs offices** (NSW Fair Trading, Consumer Affairs Victoria, Office of Fair Trading QLD, Consumer Protection WA, CBS SA, CBOS TAS, Access Canberra, NT Consumer Affairs): co-administer ACL with ACCC, regulate residential tenancy, motor dealers, real estate agents, building disputes.
- **State EPAs**: Environment protection authorities, scope varies by state.
- **State revenue offices** (Revenue NSW, SRO VIC, etc.): payroll tax, stamp duty, land tax.
- **WorkSafe / SafeWork bodies**: WHS regulators (NSW SafeWork, WorkSafe VIC, etc.). Note: model WHS laws adopted in all jurisdictions except VIC (which has its own OHS Act).
- **State law societies and bar associations**: regulate the legal profession in the state. See `legal-profession.md`.
- **State anti-discrimination boards**: Anti-Discrimination NSW, VEOHRC (VIC), QHRC, Equal Opportunity Commission WA, etc. State Acts overlay federal discrimination law.

## Watch-list for reg-feed monitoring

When configuring a regulatory feed watcher for Australia, the following sources cover the bulk of regulatory activity:

- ASIC media releases and consultation papers
- ACCC media releases and consultation papers
- OAIC media releases, determinations, consultation papers
- APRA letters, prudential standards, consultation packages
- AUSTRAC consultations and guidance
- ATO Tax Determinations, Practical Compliance Guidelines, public rulings
- Federal Register of Legislation (new and amending Acts and instruments)
- Treasury consultations
- Department-led consultations relevant to the practice area
- State and territory parliamentary bills tracker (each parliament publishes one)
- Law Council of Australia submissions and policy papers
- Federal Court daily decisions list (austlii.edu.au)
