# AGLC4 Quick Reference

> [!CAUTION]
> **AI-generated quick reference, not a substitute for the AGLC itself.** The Australian Guide to Legal Citation (4th edition, 2018) is the authoritative source. This file is a working aide-memoire. Verify any specific rule against the AGLC before relying on it for filed work.

The Australian Guide to Legal Citation (AGLC4) is the standard citation style for Australian legal academic writing, most law journals, and many courts (including the High Court). Some judges and firms have house styles that depart from AGLC. Always check the house style for filed work.

Reference: Melbourne University Law Review Association and Melbourne Journal of International Law, [*Australian Guide to Legal Citation*](https://law.unimelb.edu.au/mulr/aglc) (4th ed, 2018).

## Cases

### Reported case
Format: *Case name* (Year) Volume Reporter Page, Pinpoint (Judge).

Example: *Mabo v Queensland (No 2)* (1992) 175 CLR 1, 42 (Brennan J).

- Case name in italics.
- Parties separated by ` v ` (not `v.` and not `vs`).
- Year in round brackets if the volume number is essential to locating the report; square brackets if the year is essential (rare in Australian reporters).
- Authorised reporter preferred over unauthorised (CLR over ALR for High Court; FCR over FCA single-judge medium neutral for Federal Court when reported).

### Unreported case (medium neutral citation)
Format: *Case name* [Year] Court Number, Pinpoint (Judge).

Example: *Smith v Jones* [2024] NSWSC 123, [45] (Smith J).

- Year in square brackets.
- Court abbreviation: HCA, FCA, FCAFC, FCFCOA, NSWSC, NSWCA, VSC, VSCA, QSC, QCA, WASC, WASCA, SASC, SASCFC, TASSC, TASSCFC, ACTSC, ACTCA, NTSC, NTCA.
- Paragraph numbers in square brackets, no `p` or `para`.

### Subsequent reference
Short form after first use: drop year and reporter if unambiguous (e.g. *Mabo*).

### Pinpointing
Always pinpoint. Page numbers for reported cases. Paragraph numbers in square brackets `[45]` for unreported and medium neutral.

## Statutes

### Commonwealth
Format: *Title of Act* Year (Cth) Section.

Example: *Corporations Act 2001* (Cth) s 180(1).

### State or Territory
Format: *Title of Act* Year (State Abbreviation) Section.

Example: *Civil Liability Act 2002* (NSW) s 5B.

State abbreviations: NSW, Vic, Qld, WA, SA, Tas, ACT, NT. Note AGLC uses **Vic** (not VIC) and **Tas** (not TAS) in citations, even though regulator and statute lists often use the upper-case forms.

### Pinpointing statutes
- Section: `s 5`
- Subsection: `s 5(1)`
- Paragraph within subsection: `s 5(1)(a)`
- Multiple sections: `ss 5-7`
- Multiple non-consecutive: `ss 5, 7, 9`
- Schedule: `sch 2`
- Part: `pt IV`
- Division: `div 3`

### Delegated legislation
Format: *Title of Instrument* Year (Jurisdiction) Provision.

Example: *Corporations Regulations 2001* (Cth) reg 7.1.04.

### Bills
Format: *Title of Bill* Year (Jurisdiction).

Example: *Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Bill 2024* (Cth).

## Secondary sources

### Books
Format: Author, *Title* (Publisher, Edition, Year).

Example: J D Heydon, *Cross on Evidence* (LexisNexis Butterworths, 12th ed, 2020).

### Journal articles
Format: Author, 'Title' (Year) Volume(Issue) *Journal* Page.

Example: Megan Davis, 'Listening but Not Hearing' (2017) 39(3) *Sydney Law Review* 423.

### Reports and government publications
Author or institution, *Title* (Series number, Year) pinpoint.

Example: Australian Law Reform Commission, *Privacy* (Report No 22, 1983).

### Online sources (general)
Format: Author, 'Title', *Website* (Web Page, Date) <URL>.

Example: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, 'Australian Privacy Principles guidelines', *OAIC* (Web Page, July 2019) <https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines>.

## Footnotes and pinpoints

- AGLC uses footnotes, not in-text citations.
- Footnote numbers go after punctuation.
- First citation: full form. Subsequent: short form (using `Ibid` for immediately preceding, with pinpoint variation noted; or short form with `n` reference to first cite).
- Pinpoint in same footnote: comma then page or paragraph.

## Differences from US Bluebook

| Bluebook | AGLC |
|---|---|
| *Smith v. Jones*, 123 F.3d 456 (9th Cir. 2020) | *Smith v Jones* (2020) 123 F 3d 456 (or medium neutral) |
| 28 U.S.C. § 1331 (2018) | *Judiciary Act 1903* (Cth) s 39B (analogous AU example) |
| Id. at 45 | Ibid 45 |
| supra note 5, at 45 | Smith (n 5) 45 |
| ¶ 12 | [12] |
| pp. 45-67 | 45-67 (no `pp`) |
| § / §§ | s / ss |

## When in doubt

The AGLC is published free on the Melbourne University Law Review website. If a citation form matters (filed work, journal submission, formal correspondence), consult the AGLC directly rather than relying on this summary or on Claude's recall of citation conventions.
